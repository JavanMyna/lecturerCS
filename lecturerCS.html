A few things that are not obvious at first glance.

What is inside one block?

Take CPT111/CCA101, then NHS/NHAHM, then (DK G31).

  • The first line is the course code. A slash joins the older code and the new-curriculum code (CCA or CKC) of the same course.
  • The second line is the lecturers' initials. Two sets of initials mean two lecturers share the course.
  • The last line is the venue. DK is Dewan Kuliah (lecture hall). ELL, Lab 3, Lab 4, Lab 5 and Auditorium are specific rooms.
  • A block that spans two columns is a two-hour slot.
The grey blocks and odd labels
  • Grey Minor Sains, Minor Pengurusan/Sastera and Minor PP S.Komp slots are for students taking a minor. Skip them if you are not.
  • KO-K is Kokurikulum (co-curricular activities), on Monday and Thursday evenings.
  • WUS101, SHE102, SEA205E, Bahasa Malaysia IV and Bahasa Inggeris are university-wide courses, not School of Computer Sciences ones.
  • Bengkel Siswa Lestari (Tahun I) is the first-year workshop, on Wednesday in DK G31.
  • Ceramah Kerjaya is a career talk in the Auditorium on Thursday.
